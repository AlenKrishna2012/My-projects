MODEL ACCURACY

Planet positions use Astronomy Engine. Asteroid and comet positions use approximate Keplerian orbital elements. Spacecraft paths are historical-event reconstructions, not exact navigation ephemerides.

Planet spacing and sizes are visually compressed or exaggerated. Earth-orbiting and Moon-orbiting spacecraft are displayed with enlarged local offsets so they can be seen.
